## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI analytics project developed to analyze a medical insurance portfolio and generate insights into member demographics, claims, medical costs, premiums, health risk, insurance plans, regions, and provider networks.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow covering data preparation, transformation, DAX-based KPI development, data modeling, dashboard development, analysis, evaluation, and business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to transform medical insurance data into an interactive analytical solution that supports data-driven decisions around member management, claims, healthcare costs, insurance plans, and health-risk monitoring.

The analysis focuses on:

- Member population and demographics
- Insurance premiums
- Medical costs
- Claims paid
- Claims-to-premium performance
- Health-risk segmentation
- Chronic conditions
- BMI and health indicators
- Smoking status
- Insurance plans
- Regional performance
- Provider network performance

## 🔎 Key Findings

- The dataset contains **100,000 members**.
- **36,781 members (36.8%)** are classified as high risk.
- Annual medical costs total approximately **$300.95 million**, with an average medical cost of approximately **$3,009 per member**.
- Annual premiums total approximately **$58.23 million**.
- Claims paid total approximately **$137.79 million**.
- Current smokers have an approximately **72.7% high-risk rate**, compared with approximately **32.0% among never smokers** and **31.3% among former smokers**.
- The **Central region** has an approximately **37.4% high-risk proportion**.
- The **South region** has approximately **28,029 members** and records the largest aggregate medical cost in the project analysis.
- The analysis highlights differences in medical costs, claims, and risk across member, plan, regional, and network segments.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Strengthen early identification and monitoring** of high-risk members to support proactive health-risk management.

2. **Use targeted preventive-health and wellness initiatives** for member segments showing elevated health-risk indicators.

3. **Monitor claims, medical costs, and premiums together** to identify segments requiring closer financial and operational review.

4. **Use BMI, chronic conditions, age, smoking status, and other health indicators** to strengthen member risk segmentation.

5. **Compare regional and network performance regularly** to identify concentrations in claims, costs, and health risk.

6. **Review insurance plan performance** using member volume, premiums, claims, and medical costs to support data-driven portfolio decisions.

## 🧠 CRISP-DM Methodology

This project follows the **CRISP-DM (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)** framework.

### 1. Business Understanding

Defined the insurance performance, claims, cost, member, and health-risk questions that the analysis needed to address.

### 2. Data Understanding

Explored demographic, health, insurance, claims, financial, regional, and provider-network variables.

### 3. Data Preparation

Cleaned and transformed the dataset using Power Query and prepared analytical categories required for the dashboard.

### 4. Modeling

Developed DAX measures, KPIs, and analytical categories in Power BI to support insurance performance and risk analysis.

### 5. Evaluation

Evaluated member population, premiums, medical costs, claims, health risk, insurance plans, regions, and provider networks.

### 6. Deployment

Developed an interactive five-page Power BI dashboard designed to support insurance portfolio monitoring and data-driven decision-making.
